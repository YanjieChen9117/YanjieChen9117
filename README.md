I am a Research Staff Associate in the [Dinh Lab](https://dinhngockhanh.github.io/) at Columbia's [Irving Institute for Cancer Dynamics](https://cancerdynamics.columbia.edu/). My work applies statistics and machine learning to cancer genomics — in particular, clustering algorithms for bulk DNA sequencing. I also collaborate with the [Qin Lab](https://www.mskcc.org/profile/li-xuan-qin) at Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center on generative models for RNA and miRNA-seq.

---

### Research

- Probabilistic modeling of tumor evolution
- Subclonal inference from bulk DNA sequencing
- Generative models for genomic data
- Scalable algorithms for large-scale multi-omic datasets
